# Step 005 — The flicker on partner entries, and its owner

## Goal

Render the translate flicker on settled partner entries in `StreamRecord.tsx`, switching
the body between original and translation. Discard a partner entry's client-side
translation after it is edited. Have `SessionStream.tsx` own one `TranslationState` per
mount and dispose it on unmount (D13–D15).

## Interface intent

**`src/app/StreamRecord.tsx`** (changed)

- **`StreamRecord`** gains an **optional** prop: the `TranslationState`. It passes the
  prop to each `StreamEntry`. Without it, no entry renders a flicker, so existing callers
  and tests that do not pass one are unaffected.
- **`StreamEntry`**, for `kind === "partner"` entries and only when a translation state is
  given:
  - **The flicker:** a `shared/IconButton` with `IconLanguage`, in the entry's actions
    group next to 014's Edit. Its label follows the view derivation (`004`): "Show
    translation" for original, "Cancel translation" for pending, "Show original" for
    translated (`005.context.md`). It has a distinct colour while translated (D15). On
    click it calls `flickTranslation` for the entry's id.
  - **The body:** when the view is `"translated"`, the partner body renders the
    translated text in place of the original, through the same body renderer. Otherwise it
    renders the original, which includes the pending case. The in-place editor, when
    open, always edits the stored original text.
  - **Edit invalidation:** when the entry's commit hands a partner entry's draft to 014's
    `editEntry` and that effect settles, `invalidateTranslation` is called for the entry's
    id, whatever the outcome (D14). `editEntry`'s signature is unchanged.
- Turn and decision entries are unchanged: no flicker (US-131, brief Out).

**`src/app/SessionStream.tsx`** (changed)

- Creates one `TranslationState` per mount, the same way it creates its `StreamState`,
  and passes it to `StreamRecord`. On unmount it calls `disposeTranslations`.

## Definition of done

1. `[test]` A record of `[partner "They wave.", turn "I wave back.", decision "((skip))"]`
   is rendered with a translation state. The partner entry has a "Show translation"
   button, and the turn and decision entries have none. (brief Out, US-131)
2. `[test]` Pressing "Show translation" issues exactly one POST to
   `/api/messages/<partner id>/translation`. While it is unresolved:
   - the control is labelled "Cancel translation";
   - the body still shows "They wave.".

   After it answers text "Они машут." the body shows "Они машут.", "They wave." is no
   longer shown in that entry, and the control reads "Show original". (US-045.AC-1)
3. `[test]` Pressing "Show original" shows "They wave." again with no request. Pressing
   "Show translation" again shows "Они машут." with **no** further request: there was one
   POST in total. (UC-040, US-046.AC-1)
4. `[test]` A 502 `translation_failed` answer leaves "They wave." showing, and the control
   reads "Show translation". `notifyFailure` was called once, with an `ApiError` of code
   `translation_failed`. Pressing again issues a second POST. (US-048.AC-1, US-048.AC-2)
5. `[test]` Pressing "Cancel translation" while pending aborts the request's signal. "They
   wave." stays, the control reads "Show translation", and `notifyFailure` is not called.
   (US-133.AC-2 user-visible half, D4)
6. `[test]` Edit after translation:
   - with the translation showing, "Edit entry" opens the editor holding "They wave.";
   - typing "They bow." and blurring issues the PATCH (answered with the edited settled
     row);
   - afterwards the entry shows "They bow." (not "Они машут.") and the control reads
     "Show translation";
   - pressing it issues a **new** POST, and its answer is shown.

   (US-111.AC-2, US-111.AC-1 client half, D14)
7. `[test]` `StreamRecord` rendered **without** a translation state shows no "Show
   translation" control on a partner entry. Edit stays available. (D13, optional prop)
8. `[test]` `SessionStream` mounted for a session whose `GET …/entries` returns a partner
   row shows "Show translation" on it. Unmounting it while a translation POST is pending
   aborts that request's signal, and nothing is notified. (D13)
9. `[test]` The existing 013 / 014 `StreamRecord` and `SessionStream` tests pass. They
   are amended only as `005.context.md` permits. (013 / 014 regressions)
10. `[manual/live]` In a browser with a real model:
    - the flicker is revealed with the other entry actions on hover and on keyboard focus;
    - its colour visibly differs while the translation is shown;
    - a real partner block translates into the preferred language;
    - "Cancel translation" mid-call leaves the original with no notice;
    - a failure (model server stopped) shows the reason for about 5 s.

    (US-045.AC-1, US-048.AC-1, D15)
