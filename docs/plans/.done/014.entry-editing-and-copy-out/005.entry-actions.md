# Step 005 — Settled-entry actions: reveal, in-place edit, copy on turns

## Goal

Extend 013's `app/StreamRecord.tsx` so each settled entry's header holds one actions group
— **"Edit entry"** on every kind, **"Copy as plain text"** on turns only, and 013's
re-open on the last entry — revealed on hover or focus-within through Mantine hooks (D6,
D7); edit swaps the body for a plain textarea that saves on blur through `editEntry` (D10);
and amend 013's "no Copy anywhere" test to the new rule.

## Interface intent

**`app/StreamRecord.tsx`** (edited — 013 `004`)

- **`StreamRecord`**: unchanged props (`state`, optional `signal`), unchanged list ("Settled
  record"), kind labels, bodies, empty-record line and re-open rule. Each list item is now
  rendered by a per-entry component.
- **`StreamEntry`** (may be module-private): `observer` component for one entry, keyed by
  the entry's id. Props: the state, the entry, whether it is the last entry, and the
  signal.
  - **Reveal.** Uses `@mantine/hooks` `useHover` and `useFocusWithin`, refs merged with
    `useMergedRef` onto the entry's list item, and `useMediaQuery` for `(hover: none)`.
    Revealed = hovered **or** focus within **or** a no-hover device. The header's actions
    group carries `data-revealed` `"true"` / `"false"` and the style prop `opacity` 1 / 0;
    it is **always rendered** — controls are never hidden by `display`, `visibility`,
    conditional rendering or `disabled` for the purpose of the reveal (D6).
  - **Actions group**, in this order:
    - **"Edit entry"** (`IconEdit`, `IconButton`) on every kind — **absent while this
      entry's editor is open**;
    - **"Copy as plain text"** (`IconCopy`, `IconButton`) on `turn` entries only — absent
      on `partner` and `decision`; pressing it calls `copyAsPlainText` (`004`) with the
      entry's **stored** text (not an open editor's draft);
    - 013's **"Re-open last entry"** on the last entry under 013's rule (`showsReopen`,
      disabled while `busy`), moved into this group, behaviour unchanged.
  - **Editing.** Pressing "Edit entry" replaces the entry's body (for a partner, the
    blockquote with it; for a decision, the card) with an autosizing Mantine `Textarea`
    labelled **"Edit entry text"**, holding the entry's stored text, focused. The editing
    flag and draft are component-local `useState`. **Blur** calls `editEntry` (`004`) with
    the state, the entry's id, the draft and the signal: true → the editor closes and the
    body shows the entry as it now is in `state.entries`; false → the editor stays open
    with the typed text.
  - The body still renders by **kind** (013 D5): `partner` plain in a blockquote, `turn`
    plain, `decision` the card — an edited entry is never re-parsed.

## Definition of done

1. `[test]` With entries `[partner "P1", turn "T1", decision "((D1))"]`, each of the three
   list items holds exactly one "Edit entry" control. (US-110.AC-1, US-109.AC-1)
2. `[test]` "Copy as plain text" is present in the `turn` item and **absent** — not
   disabled — in the `partner` and `decision` items. (US-123.AC-1, US-033.AC-1, D7)
3. `[test]` (amends 013 `004` DoD-9 in `tests/app/StreamRecord.test.tsx`) No control whose
   accessible name contains "Copy" exists inside a `decision` item or a `partner` item;
   the assertion that none exists **anywhere** in the record is removed. 013's other
   `StreamRecord` tests pass unchanged — in particular DoD-10 and DoD-11 (re-open present
   only in the last item, pressing it POSTs re-open; absent with a zone row).
   (US-123.AC-1, D6, D7)
4. `[test]` Pressing "Edit entry" on the `turn` item shows a textbox "Edit entry text"
   holding "T1", focused, and the item's "Edit entry" control is gone while it is open;
   changing it to "T1 fixed" and blurring sends exactly `PATCH /api/messages/<turn id>`
   with `{"text":"T1 fixed"}`; after the served row arrives the editor is gone and the
   item shows the served text; no GET is made; the other two items' texts are unchanged.
   (US-032.AC-1, US-110.AC-1, D10)
5. `[test]` Editing the `partner` item to "P1 fixed" and blurring sends the PATCH; the
   served text renders inside a `blockquote`. Editing the `decision` item to
   "plain words" and blurring renders the served `decision` row as the out-of-character
   card (`data-paren="ooc"`) containing "plain words". (US-109.AC-1, US-110.AC-1, R12)
6. `[test]` Blurring with the text unchanged, or with only whitespace, makes no request;
   the editor closes and the stored text shows. (D10)
7. `[test]` A PATCH answered with 409 `message_not_editable` calls `notifyFailure` with
   that code, re-reads `GET /api/sessions/<id>/entries`, and — the entry still served —
   leaves the editor open holding the typed text. (D10, R10)
8. `[test]` Pressing "Copy as plain text" on a turn whose text is
   `"# Scene\n\n*She smiles* and **waits**."` writes exactly
   `"Scene\n\nShe smiles and waits."` through a stubbed `navigator.clipboard.writeText`,
   makes no `fetch` call, and calls no `notifyFailure`. (US-124.AC-1, US-033.AC-1,
   UC-030, UC-082)
9. `[test]` Reveal: at rest an item's actions group has `data-revealed="false"`;
   `mouseEnter` on the item → `"true"`; `mouseLeave` → `"false"`; focusing the item's
   "Edit entry" button (keyboard reach) → `"true"`, and blurring it to outside the item →
   `"false"`. While hidden, the item's controls are in the document, not disabled, and
   reachable by role and name. (D6)
10. `[test]` While an entry's editor is open its actions group reports
    `data-revealed="true"` (focus is within). (D6)
11. `[manual/live]` In a browser with 012, 013 and step `001` delivered: entry actions are
    invisible until the pointer is over the entry; tabbing through the record makes each
    entry's actions appear as focus enters it; on a touch device (or with hover emulation
    off) they are always visible. Edit a turn settled weeks ago (or the oldest entry),
    blur — the change survives a reload and the record does not scroll away. Copy a turn
    with markdown and paste it into Discord or an RP site's text box — no `*`, `#`, `>` or
    link syntax arrives. Open the app by its LAN address (not `localhost`) and copy again
    — the fallback puts the text on the clipboard. A decision and a partner block show no
    copy control. No notification appears on a successful save or copy.
    (US-032.AC-1, US-109.AC-1, US-110.AC-1, US-124.AC-1, US-033.AC-1, US-123.AC-1, D6, D9)
