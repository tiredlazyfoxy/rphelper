# Step 005 — Record-keeping write paths: degraded session refresh and the coverage flag on the wire

## Goal

Make settle, re-open, settled-entry text edit and partner filing refresh the session's
`session_vec` in their own transaction, in the **degraded** posture. Carry the outcome as
`search_coverage_incomplete` on the service results and on `SettleResponse`,
`ReopenResponse` and `MessageResponse`. Zone appends and zone edits do no vector work and
report `false` (`context.md` D5, D8, Wire contract).

## Interface intent

**`app/services/settle.py`**

- **`SettleResult`** and **`ReopenResult`** gain a boolean `search_coverage_incomplete`.
- **`settle`** and **`reopen`** keep their positional inputs. They gain keyword-only
  `client_factory` and `timeout_seconds` (D9 defaults). Inside the existing transaction,
  after the relational writes, each calls `003`'s `refresh_session_vector_degraded` for the
  session. The returned boolean goes onto the result. Their existing refusals (`zone_empty`,
  `zone_not_empty`, `nothing_to_reopen`, …) still happen before any refresh.

**`app/services/messages.py`**

- **`StreamMessage`** gains a boolean `search_coverage_incomplete` that **defaults to
  false**, so existing constructions and list reads are unaffected.
- **`file_partner_entry`**: same positional inputs, plus the two keyword-only parameters.
  After the insert and inside its transaction, it runs the degraded refresh. The flag goes
  onto the returned message.
- **`edit_message_text`**: same positional inputs, plus the two keyword-only parameters.
  - When the edited row is a **record row**, it runs the degraded refresh after the update,
    inside the same transaction, and the flag goes onto the result.
  - For a **zone row**, it does nothing extra, and the flag is false.
  - A buried row is still refused before any write.
- **`append_message`**: unchanged, with no vector work. Its result's flag is the default
  false.

**`app/models/stream.py`**

- `SettleResponse`, `ReopenResponse` and `MessageResponse` each gain
  `search_coverage_incomplete: bool`. Each is built from the service result's attribute.
  For `MessageResponse`, it is always present and false where the source carries the
  default.

**`app/routers/stream.py`**

- The settle, re-open, partner-filing (`POST /api/sessions/{id}/entries`) and edit
  (`PATCH /api/messages/{id}`) handlers depend on the shared `get_llm_client_factory` and
  `get_settings`, and pass the factory and `settings.llm_request_timeout_seconds` through.
- Every other handler is unchanged. The handlers stay sync `def`.

## Definition of done

1. `[test]` **Settle, model present.** The zone holds an assistant candidate "Hello
   there.". After `settle` with a designated model, the result's flag is false. The
   session's `session_vec` row equals the fake's vector for `003`'s composed text, which
   now ends with the settled entry's stored text. `message_fts` finds that text. (R11, D5)
2. `[test]` **Settle, unavailable.** With **no** designated model, `settle` succeeds:
   - the entry is settled (visible in `settled_entries`) and the zone is empty;
   - the flag is true;
   - no `session_vec` row was created, or a pre-existing one is byte-identical.

   With a fake raising `LlmUnreachableError` the outcome is the same. (US-112.AC-1, D8)
3. `[test]` **Re-open.** With a model, after a settle then `reopen`, the flag is false, and
   the row equals the fake's vector for a composed text that **no longer contains** the
   re-opened entry's text. With no model, `reopen` succeeds and its flag is true. (R11, D5)
4. `[test]` **Settled edit.** With a model, `edit_message_text` on a settled entry from
   "Old words" to "New words" returns flag false. The row equals the fake's vector for a
   composed text containing "New words" and not "Old words". The same applies to a settled
   partner block, a turn and a decision. (US-110.AC-2, US-109.AC-2)
5. `[test]` **Settled edit, no model.** The edit saves: the stored text is "New words" and
   the returned message carries it. The flag is true, and the pre-existing `session_vec`
   row is unchanged. (US-112.AC-1, US-112.AC-2)
6. `[test]` **Zone writes do no vector work.** With a designated model and a recording fake
   factory, `append_message` to the zone and `edit_message_text` on a zone row make
   **zero** factory calls. They return flag false, and leave the `session_vec` row
   unchanged. (US-115, D5)
7. `[test]` **Partner filing.** With a model, `file_partner_entry` returns flag false, and
   the composed text embedded contains the filed text. With no model, the entry is filed
   (settled, kind partner) and the flag is true. (US-121.AC-1 unchanged, US-112.AC-1)
8. `[test]` **Wire**, through `create_app()` with overrides. With no designated model:
   - `POST` settle answers 200 with `search_coverage_incomplete: true` alongside
     `entry_id`, `kind`, `buried_ids`;
   - re-open answers 200 with `true`;
   - `PATCH /api/messages/{id}` on a settled row answers 200 with `true`;
   - `POST /api/sessions/{id}/entries` answers its success status with `true`;
   - a zone append answers with `false`.

   With a designated model and the fake factory override, the same settle answers `false`.
   `SettleResponse`, `ReopenResponse` and `MessageResponse` each declare the boolean
   field. (US-112.AC-2, Wire contract)
9. `[test]` **Regression fallout.** Existing tests in `test_settle_service.py`,
   `test_messages_service.py`, `test_stream_router.py` and `test_stream_models.py` that
   fail only because the results and responses gained the field pass again with the
   field accepted. Their other assertions (`entry_id`, `kind`, `buried_ids`,
   `reopened_id`, `restored_ids`, message fields) are unchanged. (`context.md` Regression
   fallout)
