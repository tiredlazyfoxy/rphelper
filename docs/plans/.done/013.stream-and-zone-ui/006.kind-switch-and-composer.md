# Step 006 — The kind switch and the composer

## Goal

Add `app/KindSwitch.tsx` (the two-position switch showing the effective position, D8) and
`app/Composer.tsx` (the auto-growing text area, labelled Send and Settle, the settle
preview line, the partner-paste filing, and the Discard control present only for an empty
zone with a blank composer — D1, D2, D3, D6, D7, D9).

## Interface intent

**`app/KindSwitch.tsx`** (new)

- **`KindSwitch`**: `observer` component. Props: `state` (`StreamState`). A Mantine
  `SegmentedControl` labelled **"Entry kind"** with two segments, **"Partner"**
  (`"partner"`) and **"My turn"** (`"turn"`), no icons. Its value is
  `effectiveKind(state)`; choosing a segment calls `chooseKind`. Makes no request.

**`app/Composer.tsx`** (new)

- **`Composer`**: `observer` component. Props: `state` (`StreamState`) and an optional
  `signal` (`AbortSignal`) passed on to the effects it calls. Lays out, in the stream's
  column (`maw={720}`, centred, `px={18}` — style props):
  - a Mantine `Textarea` labelled **"Composer"** with `autosize`, a `minRows` giving about
    42px, no `maxRows`, no resize handle; its value is `state.draft`, changes call
    `setDraft` (D9);
  - **paste handling**: when the effective position is *partner*, a paste is intercepted —
    default insertion prevented, the clipboard's plain text passed to `filePastedPartner`
    (`003`); the draft is untouched. On *my turn* a paste behaves normally (D7);
  - the **preview line** under the text area: when `settlePreviewOf(state)` (`002`) is
    non-null, exactly one of the three fixed sentences — **"Settles as a decision (out of
    character)."** for a decision, **"Settles as a turn; the (( )) instructions will be
    removed."** for a stripping turn, **"Settles as a turn."** otherwise; when null, no
    preview line (D1);
  - a labelled secondary button **"Send"**, enabled iff `canSend(state)`, calling
    `sendComposer` (D6, D7);
  - a labelled primary button **"Settle"**, enabled iff `canSettle(state)` — so always
    disabled on *partner* (disabled, not hidden) — calling `settleComposer` (D2);
  - when `showsDiscard(state)`, an `IconButton` (`IconX`) labelled **"Discard empty
    zone"** calling `discardZone`; **absent** otherwise (D3).
  - No stop control, no copy control, no model picker.

## Definition of done

1. `[test]` `KindSwitch` with entries whose last partner-or-turn is a `turn` shows the
   "Partner" radio checked; with the last a `partner` followed by two decisions, "My
   turn" checked; with no entries, "My turn" checked. (US-120.AC-1, US-120.AC-2,
   US-120.AC-3, D8)
2. `[test]` Choosing "Partner" on `KindSwitch` sets the state's effective position to
   `"partner"` and makes no request. (D8)
3. `[test]` The composer textbox "Composer" reflects typing into `state.draft`. On *my
   turn*, with an empty zone and a blank draft, "Settle" is disabled and "Send" is
   disabled; after typing "Hello" both are enabled. (D2, D6)
4. `[test]` On *my turn* with one zone row whose role is `user`, a blank draft and no
   assistant row, "Settle" is enabled; pressing it POSTs only `…/settle` and then
   re-reads entries and zone. (US-135.AC-1, US-126.AC-2)
5. `[test]` On *my turn* with draft "My reply", pressing "Settle" POSTs
   `…/zone/messages` with `{"text":"My reply"}` and then `…/settle`; the composer is
   empty afterwards. (D2, US-031.AC-1)
6. `[test]` On *partner*, "Settle" is present and disabled even with zone rows and a
   non-blank draft. (D2, `workspace-shell.md`)
7. `[test]` On *partner*, pasting "Partner text ((ooc?))" into the composer POSTs
   `…/entries` with exactly `{"kind":"partner","text":"Partner text ((ooc?))"}` with no
   Settle press, and the composer's value is unchanged (still its previous draft). The
   same happens with zone rows present. (US-121.AC-1, US-030.AC-1, US-121.AC-2, D7)
8. `[test]` On *my turn*, a paste makes no request. (D7)
9. `[test]` On *partner* with draft "Typed partner text", pressing "Send" POSTs
   `…/entries` with `{"kind":"partner","text":"Typed partner text"}`; on *my turn* with
   draft "Discussion line", "Send" POSTs `…/zone/messages` with
   `{"text":"Discussion line"}`. (D6, D7)
10. `[test]` Preview line: on *my turn* with draft `"((OOC aside))"` it reads exactly
    "Settles as a decision (out of character)."; with draft
    `"She walks. ((make it tense)) He waits."` exactly "Settles as a turn; the (( ))
    instructions will be removed."; with draft "She walks." exactly "Settles as a turn.";
    with a blank draft and last zone row `"((note))"` the decision sentence (the target
    is the last zone row). On *partner*, or with a blank draft and an empty zone, no
    preview sentence is shown. (D1, US-129.AC-1, US-130.AC-1, US-126.AC-1)
11. `[test]` "Discard empty zone" is present with an empty zone and a blank composer;
    absent once the composer holds "x"; absent with one zone row (not disabled in either
    case). (US-134.AC-1, US-134.AC-2, D3)
12. `[test]` With an override of *partner* on an empty zone, pressing "Discard empty
    zone" makes no request and returns the switch to its computed default. (UC-086, D3)
13. `[test]` While a Send is pending, "Send" and "Settle" are disabled. (D15)
14. `[manual/live]` In a browser the composer starts about two lines (≈42px) tall, has no
    resize handle, grows with each new line with no internal scrollbar until the viewport
    runs out, and shares the stream's left edge at 720px / 18px. (D9,
    `workspace-shell.md` geometry)
